Key readings

Rules-based facts on weekly closes; no published view.

Zones

Each stock is measured against the benchmark of its zone, in the benchmark's currency, price return on both sides. Click a zone to filter the screen.

Zone × sector

Share of the stocks in the cell; the small figure is the number of stocks. Cells with fewer than three stocks are greyed. Click a cell to filter the screen.

Screen

Filter, sort by any column, search a name or a ticker. Click a stock for its weekly chart; the arrows move through the list as filtered. ★ or ★★ at the right of a row adds the company to your short list.

My short list

The companies you flagged in the screen, two stars first, then one star. Click a lit star again to remove a company. Buy sends a company to the Focus List workspace as a proposed purchase. The list is kept in this browser.

Focus List

The Focus List names, weakest readings first. Click Sell or Increase at the right of a row; click a name for its chart.

Sells and buys

Sells and increases come from the Focus List table above; buys come from My short list in the Master list workspace. Click ✕ to cancel a line.

The Focus List is read as equally weighted. An increase is recorded here but does not change a weight in the breakdown.

Optimiser

Proposals from the short list

Optional. It takes the Focus List after your sells and buys, and proposes additions from My short list.
