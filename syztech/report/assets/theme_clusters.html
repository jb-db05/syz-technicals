Method

Prices only. The common equity-market move is removed first, so themes are grouped on what is specific to them.

1Remove the market

Weekly returns in USD over three years. Each theme's beta to the MSCI ACWI is taken out; what is left is its own behaviour.

2Group what moves together

Themes whose leftover returns are correlated go in the same cluster (average correlation inside a cluster above 0.45).

3Read the exposure

For each cluster: beta to the ACWI and the correlation of its leftover returns with rates, the dollar, oil, copper, gold and equity styles.

Clusters

Names are proposals – tell me which to rename, merge or split. Click a cluster to see its themes. Beta: against the MSCI ACWI. Exposure tags: correlation of the cluster's market-neutral returns with each factor, shown when it is 0.25 or more.

How to read it

    Near-duplicates

    Sets of tickers whose market-neutral returns are correlated at 0.95 or more: the same exposure listed several times (share classes, the same fund on two exchanges, a fund and its index). One line per set is enough for the review.
